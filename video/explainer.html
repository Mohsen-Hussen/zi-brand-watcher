The idea

Let a bot do the watching.

👀 Watches 24/7

Checks the store's live product feed every few minutes, in the cloud — my PC can be off.

⚡ Pings instantly

The moment a hoodie flips from “coming soon” to available, it sends a Telegram message.

🧾 Says what's live

Product, size, colour, price and a direct order link — so I just check out.

Step 1 — make the Telegram bot

Five taps in @BotFather.

1Open @BotFather and send /newbot.
2Give it a name — e.g. ZiBrand Stock Alert.
3Pick a username ending in bot.
4Copy the token it gives you  8•••••••:AAH••••••••••  — keep it secret.
5Message your bot, then grab your chat ID from /getUpdates.
Step 2 — the code

About 80 lines of Python + one workflow.

check_stock.py
# spot the "coming soon" → live flip
for p in products:
  for v in p["variants"]:
    if v["available"]:
      alert(p["title"], v["title"])
send_telegram()
send(
  f"https://api.telegram.org/
  bot{TOKEN}/sendMessage",
  chat_id=CHAT_ID,
  text="🔥 Hoodie just dropped!")
.github/workflows/stock-check.yml
on:
  schedule:
    - cron: "*/5 * * * *"   # every 5 minutes, in the cloud
